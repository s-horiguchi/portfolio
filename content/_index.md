**Shuhei A. Horiguchi (堀口 修平) is an Assistant Professor** at the Nano Life Science Institute  (NanoLSI), Kanazawa University, and a member of [Okuda Lab](http://mechgen.jp/index-en.html), led by Satoru Okuda.
I am also a PRESTO Individual Researcher at the Japan Science and Technology Agency (JST) in the research area of [Mathematical Sciences for the Future](https://www.jst.go.jp/kisoken/presto/en/research_area/area2024-2.html).
I received my Ph.D. from the University of Tokyo in March 2024 under the supervision of [Tetsuya J. Kobayashi](https://research.crmind.net).


##### Research

I am interested in exploring analogies and contrasts between artificial and biological systems.


 * Information processing in multicellular systems (e.g., immune systems, nervous systems, embryonic development, and evolution)
 * Robotics and computer science
