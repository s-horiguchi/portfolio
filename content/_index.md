**Shuhei A. Horiguchi (堀口 修平) is an assistant professor** at Nano Life Science Institute  (Nano-LSI), Kanazawa University in [OKUDA LAB](http://mechgen.jp/index-en.html) led by Satoru Okuda, and a PRESTO Individual Researcher, Japan Science and Technology Agency (JST) in the research area [Mathematical Sciences for the Future](https://www.jst.go.jp/kisoken/presto/en/research_area/area2024-2.html).
I have obtained PhD in March 2024 at the University of Tokyo under the supervision of [Tetsuya J. Kobayashi](https://research.crmind.net).

I am interested in an analogy and contrast between artificial and biological systems. 


**Research interest:**

 * Information processing in the multicellular system (e.g., immune system, nervous system, embryogenesis, and evolution of them)
 * Robotics / Computer science
